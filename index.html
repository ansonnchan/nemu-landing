Private by design

Your activity journal should not become activity surveillance.

Nemu records only the application in front, UTC timing, and idle intervals. Pairing is private, credentials stay in Keychain, and detailed cloud activity expires after 30 days.

  • ✓ No screenshots or screen recording
  • ✓ No browser history, URLs, or titles
  • ✓ No messages, documents, or input values
